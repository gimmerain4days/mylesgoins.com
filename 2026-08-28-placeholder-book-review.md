A review worth reading answers three questions: what is this book actually arguing, is the argument any good, and did it change how I think? Plot summary is what the jacket is for.

Open with the book's central claim in your own words — stated fairly enough that the author would nod along. Then the interesting part: where it's right, where it's wrong, and where it's wrong in a way that teaches you something anyway.

Close with who should read it. "Everyone" is never the answer, and "no one" almost never is.
