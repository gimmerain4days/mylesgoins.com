Every site like this one starts with a post explaining itself, so here's mine. I wanted a place to write that I own — not an account on someone's platform, but a domain with my name on it and a folder of plain text files behind it.

The plan is three kinds of writing. Thoughts on AI, because we're living through the most interesting technology shift of my lifetime and I'd like a record of what I thought while it was happening. Technical deep dives, when a problem is interesting enough to earn one. And reviews — books mostly, sometimes other media — because writing about what you read is the difference between reading and remembering.

No comments, no analytics, no newsletter popup. If you want to follow along, there's [RSS](/feed.xml).
