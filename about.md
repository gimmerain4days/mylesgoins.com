# About

I'm Myles Goins, a licensed Professional Engineer in Sacramento, California. I work in pavement and civil infrastructure design — the roads, streets, and public works that people use every day without thinking about, which is how you know they're working.

Outside of work I read widely, follow the development of AI closely, and have more hobbies than is strictly advisable. This site is where the thinking that doesn't fit anywhere else ends up.

## What to expect here

Essays on AI and its trajectory, technical deep dives when a problem earns one, and reviews of books and other media — mostly the ones that changed my mind about something, or tried to and failed in an interesting way.

## Colophon

This site is plain text at heart: posts are markdown files, built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/), hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). The colors are [Flexoki](https://stephango.com/flexoki), a palette by Steph Ango designed for reading on paper-like backgrounds. There is no tracking, no analytics, and nothing to accept or dismiss.
